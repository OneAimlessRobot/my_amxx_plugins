Part 2 of extra help file!


(console) sh_check_map_hero_blacklisted_maps - shows a list of maps that a hero cannot be played on. Requires part of hero name


(console) sh_check_map_hero_whitelisted_maps - shows a list of the only maps that a hero can be played on. Requires part of hero name


(console) sh_check_map_blacklisted_heroes - shows a list of heroes that are blacklisted for at least one map


(console) sh_check_map_whitelisted_heroes - shows a list of heroes that are whitelisted for at least one map


(console) sh_check_existing_damage_types - shows a list of the damage types that exist!


(console) sh_print_player_hero_damage_recoveries - shows a list of damage types that this player can currently recover hp from!


(console) sh_print_hero_damage_recoveries - shows a list of damage types that a hero allows a user to recover from!
